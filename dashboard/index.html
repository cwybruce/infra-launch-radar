How the number is built

Implied value = TVL × M × D × G × A × C. M is the median mcap/TVL of the live infra tokens above, computed from this snapshot. D is the infra-demand multiplier by category (AI 1.50, Chain 1.40, DOR 1.35, Restaking 1.30, Risk Curators / Allocators 1.15, Collateral Markets 1.10, everything else 1.00). G is 1 + the 7-day TVL change clamped to −30%…+60%. A is maturity: 0.85 for a new listing, 1.00 at four years tracked. C is chain breadth: 1 + 0.03 per extra chain, capped at 15.

Coverage: every protocol in the snapshot that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that trades — the full watchlist plus the early ones below the floor. “Records no token” is DefiLlama’s record, not proof a protocol has none. The implied value is this entry’s own claim, never make.fun’s, and not a prediction that a token will be issued. Farm plans are starting points: check fees and risks yourself before moving funds.
